Trenchline Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

Trenchline ("the game") is a real-time territory control strategy game published by Burak Seker ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with data. It is short, because the game itself does very little with data: it is played entirely on your device against computer opponents, it has no accounts, and it asks you for nothing. The only thing that goes to the internet is the advertising described below.

What we collect

Nothing. We have no server and receive no data from the game. We cannot see who plays, which settings you choose or how you play.

What stays on your device

The game keeps the following in the device's private app storage and sends none of it to us:

  • your saved battles: the state and result of your last 30 battles (Past Games);
  • your settings: the difficulty you chose, and whether sound, autosave, the minimap and high resolution are on.

If Android's own backup is turned on, Android may back these files up to your Google account; that backup is yours and we have no access to it.

Deleting them is up to you: Ayarlar → Tüm kayıtları sil (Settings → Delete all saves) in the game removes the saved battles, Android Settings → Apps → Trenchline → Storage → Clear data resets everything, and uninstalling the game leaves nothing behind.

Advertising

The game shows a full-screen ad as a battle starts (at most one every 3 minutes). The ads are served by Google AdMob, part of Google. To do so, the AdMob software inside the game collects and processes device data: your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and operating system information, and your interactions with ads.

That processing is Google's, not ours; we only see aggregate counts of how many ads were shown. How Google uses this data is described here:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time under Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Consent: if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland

Before requesting a single ad, the game shows you a consent form through Google's User Messaging Platform, a certified consent management platform. No ad is requested until you answer, and your answer decides whether the ads you see are personalised.

You can change your answer at any time: Ayarlar → Reklam gizlilik seçenekleri (Settings → Ad privacy options) in the game opens the form again. That option appears only where the form is shown.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Permissions

The game asks for internet access, network state, the advertising ID and Android's ad services permissions, along with the wake lock and foreground service permissions that Google's libraries use to finish background work. All of them come from the advertising software and exist only so that an ad can be fetched and counted. The game does not ask for the camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or storage.

Changes
